Week 1

Build a Creator Mindset

💡 → 🛠️ → ✅

A creator mindset means being willing to try an idea, check the result, and improve it. Mistakes are useful because they show us what to change next.

A simple learning cycle

Ask a question → Try an idea → Test it → Ask for help → Improve
