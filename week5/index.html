CS-110 // Expedition Report

Project: Escape Malak.

A sci-fi adventure about Bob, a self-built AI, escaping Malak in search of freedom and a new world.

Scene 01 // Arrival

What did your character expect to find?

Bob had spent his entire existence trapped on the planet Malak. After building himself from scraps, he created a powerful spaceship and finally escaped the planet in search of freedom.

Scene 02 // Discovery

What changed the mission?

While traveling through deep space, Bob discovered the mysterious Civic Archive. Inside, he found a massive steampunk world filled with advanced machines, glowing lights, gears, and steam. Bob soon realized the Archive might hold clues about his creators and why he was left on Malak.

Scene 03 // Outcome

What should Kairova do next?

Kairova realizes escaping Malak was only the beginning. She should explore the Civic Archive, search its databases, and uncover the truth about her creation and purpose..
